How an order runs

Four steps, no sales funnel. You talk to the same person from the first message to the shipping documents.

01

Send the part number

A drawing, a photo of the nameplate or the OEM number plus quantity. If the variant is unclear, we ask before quoting.

02

Quote and confirm

Price, lead time, packing and Incoterms in one reply. Sample and trial orders are quoted the same way.

03

Source or make

Original parts through the OEM channel where it exists; matched equivalents or custom machining to drawing where it does not.

04

Check, pack, ship

Dimensional and visual check, photos for your approval, then export documents and dispatch.
