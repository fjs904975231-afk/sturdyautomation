About Sturdy Automation

One partner for automation, electrical and instrumentation spares.

Sturdy Automation is an industrial trading and supply company based in China, working with plants, OEMs, EPC contractors and system integrators worldwide. Our focus is fire and gas detection, industrial PCs, displays and monitors, PLC/HMI control hardware, drives and flow measurement — products built for the demanding conditions of modern industry.

We were set up to bridge innovation and practicality: to make advanced, reliable technology easier to specify, buy and keep running. Our customers work across oil & gas, power, manufacturing, water and infrastructure, where safety and uptime are not negotiable.

Why industry experience matters

The same part number can be the wrong part.

Enclosure rating, gas group, temperature class, hazardous-area certification, communication protocol — a missing suffix changes what a product is allowed to do. We check the details with you before the order leaves China.

  • Hazardous-area and ingress-protection ratings verified
  • Protocol and I/O compatibility confirmed against your system
  • Voltage, coil and supply variants matched to site conditions
  • Alternatives proposed when the original is end-of-life
